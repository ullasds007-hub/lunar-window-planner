Observe from Earth

Find Moon-viewing or dark-sky windows. Times are local to Mysuru; weather is not included.

Moon viewing: The Moon is at least 10° above the horizon and the Sun is at least 6° below it.
Dark sky: The Moon is below the horizon and the Sun is at least 18° below it.

Choose your dates and select Find windows.

What qualifies?

Moon viewing: Moon at least 10° above the horizon and Sun at least 6° below it. Dark sky: Moon below the horizon and Sun at least 18° below it. An observing night runs from local noon to the next noon.
